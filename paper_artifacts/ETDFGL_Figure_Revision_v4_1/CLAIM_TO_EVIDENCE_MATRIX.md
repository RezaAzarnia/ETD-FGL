### Audit Classification Scheme (Revision v4.1 Standard):
- **Verified:** Direct empirical observations, calculated quantities, algorithm formulas mapped to frozen code, verified SHA-256 hashes, and verified checkpoint files on disk.
- **Hypothesis / Conjecture:** Unverified structural explanations or conjectures where underlying causal logs were not directly recorded (e.g., DMGNN-FL edge pruning count, PubMed citation sparsity hypothesis). Unsubstantiated causal claims (such as Reddit Seed 43 degree variance causality) are excluded from manuscript claims.
- **Not verified:** Unsubstantiated generalizations, claims not supported by experimental records, or metadata absent from per-run summary JSON files (e.g., GPU hardware model).
- **Not Recorded:** Metrics where raw evaluation arrays were not serialized to scalar summary files (e.g., F1-score, Precision, Recall).
- **Not Measured / Excluded:** Metrics outside the scope of federated graph learning (e.g., Differential Privacy $\epsilon$, IoT Packet Delivery Ratio).
